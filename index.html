GOOD TO KNOW

          

Frequently asked questions.

        
        

          Need more information? Send your question directly           to our team on WhatsApp.         

      
      
        
          How do I book an apartment?           

            Complete the booking request form and continue to WhatsApp.             The team will confirm availability, apartment options and rates.           

        
        
          Does submitting a request confirm my stay?           

            No. Your reservation is confirmed only after the property             team has checked availability and explicitly confirmed             your booking.           

        
        
          How do I pay?           

            After the team confirms your reservation and the amount due,             you can use the Paystack payment link on this website.             Always verify the amount and payment details before paying.           

        
        
          Can I ask about facilities or special requests?           

            Yes. Include your questions in the booking form or contact             the team on WhatsApp. Facilities and special requests are             subject to confirmation.
